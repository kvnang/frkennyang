# About Fr. Kenny

Kenny Ang, a native of Jakarta, discerned his vocation to the priesthood as a gradual journey guided by the teachings and grace of the Catholic faith. A deep commitment to teaching is central to his intellectual life. He views this pedagogical responsibility as an essential impetus for continuous research and the refinement of his own thought, maintaining that without it, his personal studies would lack the same rigor. His overarching goal is to assist others in navigating the complexities of belief and fostering a deeper relationship with God, whom he understands to be the ultimate source of true happiness. He currently resides in Rome.
